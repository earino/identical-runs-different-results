
## Research

You may and should use web search / fetch tools (if your harness has them) to look up XGBoost tuning guides,
categorical-encoding strategies, and domain-specific feature engineering ideas before your first
non-baseline experiment and whenever you plateau (3+ consecutive discards). Cite the source in your commit
message when an idea comes from research. Research time counts against the wall-clock budget, not against
the experiment count.
