What it says

  • The harness matters as much as the model, when there is no playbook.

    On airline, same model and same budget, deepseek v4 flash ranges 0.0438 across the six agents, below baseline to best-of-pilot, wider than the spread across models inside the best agent. pi is the most consistent agent on every airline model family.

  • A public playbook collapses the harness effect.

    On Home Credit, whose winning features are on Kaggle, the finished harnesses on deepseek 4.1 flash sit within 0.0035 of each other and on glm-5.3 flash within 0.0045. What still differs is cost and waste: on glm-5.3 flash Claude Code took 256 minutes with 17 failed experiments to reach 0.7706, hermes 77 minutes for 0.7698.

  • Heavy fits turn the disclosed time limit into the discriminator.

    Credit's fits are 3.5× airline's. Claude Code walked into the 180-second limit six times in a row with oversized ensembles while pi never did; both landed at the same +0.022. The memory cap, undisclosed until now, bit once and is now in the prompt.

  • The provider flips the bottleneck.

    Share of wall time inside training: airline 0.40 on the metered provider versus 0.67 on the flat-rate gateway; credit 0.62 versus 0.70. Token speed was 13–25 versus 100–240 tokens per second.

  • Cache cost is a property of the agent, not the model.

    On deepseek v4 pro, pi cached 97% and cost $0.22; Claude Code cached 9.6% on the identical model and cost $5.71, because its context outgrows the cache between slow turns. A chatty loop is expensive twice.

  • Cost and accuracy are uncorrelated.

    Across all airline cells on Ollama the most expensive agent, Claude Code at $10.60, scored below pi at $4.02.

  • The provider changes the speed, not the answer.

    deepseek 4.1 flash under slightly different names: 5 clean airline pairs, Ollama higher every time, median +0.0115. GLM under identical names: 4 pairs, median -0.0086, a wash. The deepseek gap is a build difference, not a quality tax. Separately, glm-5.3 on LunaRoute dropped streams overnight; its credit cells are excluded as gateway failures.

  • Bigger nemotron buys nothing.

    super 120B is the sweet spot on airline at 0.7324; ultra 550B lands 0.7191–0.7243 across six agents. nano and gpt-oss 20B cannot sustain an agent loop on either dataset.

Caveats

Seed 1 only. Directional, not final. Credit is still running on both providers. Two agents independently discovered refitting the final model on train plus eval; on a temporal split that leaks the holdout's year, on credit's random split it is legitimate; flagged cells are marked. Credit and fraud are famous Kaggle datasets with public solutions, so they measure recall as much as search; airline has no such playbook. Costs are list price; LunaRoute rows ran on a flat plan.
